Frequently Asked Questions

The app isn't working or crashes

Make sure you're on the latest version from the App Store, then fully close and reopen the app. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and iOS version and we'll look into it.

How do I restore my ad-free purchase?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Restores use the Apple ID that made the original purchase.

How do leaderboards and achievements work?

Sign in with Game Center to unlock achievements, track progress across devices, and compete on global leaderboards. You can manage Game Center under the iOS Settings app.

Do I need an internet connection?

Most game modes work completely offline. An internet connection is only needed for global leaderboards and Team Challenge.

How do I change the app language?

The app follows your device language. You can override it in the iOS Settings app under the Flag Quiz entry.
