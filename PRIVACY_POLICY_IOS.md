# Privacy Policy — Guess the Flag (iOS)

**Last updated:** June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Guess the Flag" ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application on Apple iOS and iPadOS.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Player name (optional):** When creating or joining a team challenge, you may enter a display name. This name is visible to other participants during the challenge and is submitted to the online leaderboard (see "Online Leaderboard" below). You can use any nickname — we recommend not using your real name.
- **Challenge data:** When creating or joining team challenges, quiz scores, answers, and completion times are stored temporarily on our servers to enable the multiplayer experience.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Camera:** The App requests camera access solely to scan QR codes for joining team challenges. Camera data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted or stored by us.
- **Install identifier:** On first launch the App generates a random identifier. It is not linked to your real identity and is used as your key on the online leaderboard when you submit a score.

## Online Leaderboard

Guess the Flag has a global leaderboard. When you submit a score, the App sends the following to a Google Firebase Realtime Database:

- Your display name (if entered) and/or your random install identifier
- Quiz score, accuracy, and session duration
- A timestamp

Leaderboard entries are visible to other players. Submitting a score is optional — the App works fully without it.

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob

The free version of the App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (interstitial ads and rewarded video ads). Google may collect and process certain information including:

- Device identifiers, including the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) **only if you grant permission** (see "App Tracking Transparency" below)
- IP address
- General location (country/region level)
- App usage data

AdMob also uses Apple's **SKAdNetwork** to measure ad campaign performance in a privacy-preserving, aggregated way that does not identify you.

Users who purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app product no longer see ads after purchase.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### App Tracking Transparency (IDFA)

On iOS, the App will ask for your permission via Apple's **App Tracking Transparency** prompt before any advertising partner can access the IDFA to deliver personalized advertising. If you decline, the App and its ad partners will not access the IDFA, and you will receive non-personalized ads. You can change this choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

### Firebase Realtime Database (Google)

Team challenge data and leaderboard entries are stored on Google Firebase servers (EU region). See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit)

The in-app purchase "Remove Ads" is processed by Apple's App Store using StoreKit. We do not collect or receive your payment details — Apple handles the transaction and only reports the purchase status back to the App. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not require account creation or login
- We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or real names
- We do not collect precise location data
- We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or attribution SDKs beyond what AdMob provides
- We do not track users across other apps or websites without your App Tracking Transparency consent
- We do not sell any user data to third parties

## Data Storage

### On your device

App data is stored locally using iOS storage:

- Quiz scores, Hall of Fame, and statistics
- App preferences (language, theme, sound settings)
- "Remove Ads" purchase status
- Your display name and random install identifier

Uninstalling the App removes all local data.

### On our servers

Leaderboard entries and active team challenge data are stored on Google Firebase. Challenge data is deleted once the challenge ends. Leaderboard entries persist until you request removal. To request removal of your data, contact us at the email below.

## Permissions and Device Access

The App uses the following iOS capabilities:

- **Network access** — required to fetch ads from Google AdMob and to submit and read leaderboard and team challenge data.
- **Camera** — used only to scan QR codes when joining a team challenge. You can decline camera access; the challenge can also be joined by entering a code manually.
- **App Tracking Transparency** — the App asks for permission before any access to the IDFA for personalized advertising (you can decline, and revoke later in Settings).

The App does not request access to location, contacts, photos, or the microphone.

## Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only user-provided data is an optional display name shown on the leaderboard; we recommend not using a real name. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

## Your Rights

- You can delete all local data (scores, preferences, display name, identifiers, purchase status cache) by uninstalling the App.
- You can change or clear your display name at any time from the App settings.
- You can request removal of your online leaderboard data by contacting us at the email below.
- You can control ad tracking through **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** on your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
