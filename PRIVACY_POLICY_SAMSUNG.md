# Privacy Policy

**App:** Guess the Flag
**Store:** Samsung Galaxy Store
**Developer:** Ganaj
**Last updated:** May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Guess the Flag" ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application distributed through the Samsung Galaxy Store.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Player name:** When joining a team challenge, you enter a display name. This name is stored temporarily on our servers for the duration of the challenge and is visible to other participants. The name does not have to be your real name — any nickname is acceptable.
- **Challenge data:** When creating or joining team challenges, quiz scores, completion times, and answers are stored temporarily to enable the multiplayer experience.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device camera:** The App requests camera access solely to scan QR codes for joining team challenges. Camera data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted or stored.
- **Network state:** The App checks the network connection state to determine whether online features (team challenges, ads in the free version) are available.

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not require account creation or login.
- We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or real names.
- We do not collect precise location data.
- We do not track users across other apps or websites.
- We do not sell any user data to third parties.
- We do not use Samsung Account data, Samsung Health data, or any other Samsung service data.

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob (Free version only)
The free version of the App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process certain information including:
- Device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
- IP address
- General location (country/region level)
- App usage data related to ad delivery

The App is configured for a 13+ audience. Ads are non-personalized where required by law and the user has not consented to personalization. For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Firebase Realtime Database (Google LLC)
Team challenge data (challenge codes, participant display names, scores, and timestamps) is stored on Firebase Realtime Database to enable multiplayer functionality. This data is:
- Stored on servers located in the European Union (europe-west1 region).
- Retained only for the duration of the challenge and automatically purged afterwards.
- Not linked to any personal account, email, or device identifier.
- Not used for analytics or advertising purposes.

For more information, see the [Firebase Privacy and Security page](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Data Storage and Retention

| Data | Where it is stored | Retention |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| Quiz scores, Hall of Fame | Locally on your device | Until you clear app data or uninstall |
| App preferences (language, theme, music) | Locally on your device | Until you clear app data or uninstall |
| Team challenge data | Firebase RTDB (EU, europe-west1) | Duration of the challenge only |
| Advertising identifiers (free version) | Handled by Google AdMob | Per Google's policies |

We do not maintain any server of our own that stores user data beyond what Firebase provides for the live multiplayer feature.

## Permissions Used by the App

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `CAMERA` | Scan QR codes to join team challenges (on-device, not transmitted). |
| `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Multiplayer team challenges; serving ads in the free version. |
| `AD_ID` (Android 13+) | Required by Google AdMob in the free version to deliver ads and apply frequency capping. Can be reset or limited in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. |

## Children's Privacy

The App is rated and configured for users aged 13 and over and is **not** designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The team challenge feature requires only a display name (which can be any nickname) and does not collect any other personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / similar laws)

Because the App does not collect personally identifiable information tied to an account, most data subject requests do not apply. However, you have the right to:

- **Access / delete local data:** Clear the App's data via Settings → Apps → Guess the Flag, or uninstall the App.
- **Reset advertising identifier:** Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID.
- **Opt out of personalized advertising:** Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (Android 12+) or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
- **Request information or deletion of challenge data:** Contact us by email (see below). As challenge data is auto-purged after the session, normally no data is retained.

## International Users

The App is available worldwide via the Samsung Galaxy Store. Firebase data is stored in the European Union. Advertising services may be provided from outside your country (United States and other countries where Google operates). By using the App you consent to such cross-border transfers where applicable.

## Security

We use Firebase's standard security rules to ensure that challenge data is readable only by participants of the same challenge. All network communication uses HTTPS / TLS. Despite these measures, no online service can be guaranteed 100% secure.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available at the URL provided in the Samsung Galaxy Store listing. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
